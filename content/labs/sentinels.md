The lab runs the released Python analyzer on your device. It parses edited source as data and never executes your application. Every report names its inspected scope, structural evidence and unresolved analysis; a structural connection does not prove successful execution.

Alongside connections, handoffs and shapes, the coverage story inventories declarations in a selected source directory. Add a function to expose a missing contract or internal classification. Explicit internal reasons document scope decisions; they do not establish runtime correctness.
