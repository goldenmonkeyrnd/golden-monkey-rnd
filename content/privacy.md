## What is measured

Cloudflare Web Analytics measures aggregate page traffic and performance. A small application event system records conference-page views, county comparisons, source-inspector opens, GitHub clicks, résumé downloads, walkthrough starts and email-link clicks.

Each application event contains an allowlisted action, a page surface and the fixed conference campaign label. The event payload does not include your name, email, IP address, full referrer, URL query parameters or a persistent visitor identifier. The application does not set analytics cookies or fingerprint your device. Cloudflare may process network request information to provide and protect its services.

## Browser privacy preferences

When your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, the site does not load its Web Analytics beacon or send application engagement events. Essential page and data requests still work. Blocking analytics does not block the portfolio or CivicAtlas.

## What these numbers mean

Reports describe visits and actions. They do not identify recruiters or prove that an individual returned. An email-link click measures intent; it does not prove an email was sent or a meeting took place. Aggregate event reports account for Cloudflare Analytics Engine sampling.

## Contact

There is no contact form on this site. Choosing **Email Pauline** opens your email application. Any message you send is handled through Gmail and used to respond to you. Actual professional conversations are tracked separately in my private job-search records.
